# Ambient Home

The idea of ambient home is to provide an information in a calm, non-intrusive way.
The rule of thumb is "no digits, no ticking". The screen should be calm and stable.

## The sky arc

This version displays a sun moving along a sky arc, with the sun position indicating the current time of day. 
The sun is a small circle that moves along a curved path from left to right, representing the passage of time.
